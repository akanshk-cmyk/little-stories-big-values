# Interface and interaction design

Updated 6 November 2026.

My account includes Account security (password change and one-time recovery-code setup), Forgot your password? (requires a previously saved code), and Recent story requests. Continuing an interrupted request is an explicit action that warns about provider credits. Unknown outcomes are never silently retried. Recovery secrets are cleared on hiding/sign-out and are not saved in browser storage. Full account erasure is not advertised as available.

Parent details live in My account → Parent profile (name, optional email, login and creation date). On My Storybooks, a collapsed Parent account section shows the signed-in parent's name/email/login and links to editing. The child reader badge remains separate. Blank legacy profile fields say Not provided. Email is not a verified sign-in or recovery channel.

Keep one top-right Sign in / My account entry beside the reader badge. Discover, My Story Studio, Sources and My Storybooks remain. Account-backed profile editing and confirmed deletion are on the account page. Saved books stay available after a child profile is deleted. Source/adaptation notes are collapsed; completion shows PDF and My Storybooks, with no progress controls.

Original fiction increment: parents can choose Original teaching-inspired fiction, describe their own characters/adventure, and use the same account-backed QA, parent approval, saved library and text PDF path without Ramayan retrieval. Separate fiction prompts and explicit classification prevent imagined events being presented as source evidence. English, a three-minute target and the existing short-duration allowance remain; no image generation or narration is enabled. The controlled end-to-end evidence is `docs/verification/original-fiction-browser-2026-11-06.json`; live quality remains unmeasured.
