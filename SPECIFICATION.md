# Application specification

Updated 6 November 2026.

The active release accepts original teaching-inspired fiction from a parent description, or limited English Ramayan adaptations. Originals support characters from the description or a displayed character and Kindness/Sharing/Patience/Honesty; adaptations support Rama/Hanuman and Kindness/Sharing. Both use a three-minute target and the age of an owned child profile. The target is not a guaranteed reading length; the current short-story allowance is disclosed. Six ordered paragraphs must each contain at least 25 words, with at least 180 child-facing words overall and no repeated complete pages. Traditional retelling is absent from the menu. Original fiction does not retrieve Ramayan evidence; all stories still require draft QA and parent approval before saving/PDF.
